Anne Jeung started lessons at the age of seven and has never stopped learning or playing the piano since. She obtained her bachelor’s degree in Piano Performance at Queens College, City University of New York; and a Master’s Degree in Piano Performance at the Ohio State University.

She has performed and taught piano for more than 25 years in New York, Ohio, and Florida, where she presently resides. Anne is currently a member of the Orlando Music Club and Florida Federation Music Club, where her students have received Superior ratings and won competitions in scholarship events.
