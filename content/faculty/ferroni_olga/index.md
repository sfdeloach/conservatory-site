Olga Ferroni, M.M., is an acclaimed violinist, producer, and the Creative Director of Kalinka Entertainment. With a career rooted in the rigorous traditions of Eastern European classical training, Olga holds a Master’s degree in Violin Performance from G. Musicescu Conservatory in Kishinev, Moldova, and has served as a core musician with the Orlando Philharmonic Orchestra since 2001.

Beyond the orchestral stage, Olga is a recognized force in the jazz and commercial entertainment sectors. Her diverse portfolio includes collaborations with Walt Disney World Entertainment, Cirque du Soleil, and major symphonies across Florida. This versatility is matched by over 25 years of dedicated teaching experience. Her pedagogical reach spans from foundational early-childhood instruction to coaching college-level students and aspiring professionals, specializing in advanced technique and performance mindset.

Through Kalinka Entertainment, Olga leverages her deep expertise in both classical and jazz idioms to produce high-caliber musical experiences. Her work is defined by a commitment to merging diverse musical styles into a cohesive, sophisticated brand of entertainment that reflects her authoritative technique and unique signature style.
