Tammy Davis has taught students of all ages and skill levels through private lessons, group keyboard classes, and early childhood music programs in both Alabama and Florida. Her teaching experience includes community music school programs, college-affiliated music programs, and her own independent home studio.

She earned a Bachelor of Music in Piano Performance from the University of North Alabama and a Master of Music in Piano Pedagogy from the University of Montevallo. In addition, she completed certification in early childhood music education through Musikgarten.

Tammy has served as a church pianist throughout the years and has been a member of the Music Teachers National Association (MTNA) and the Florida Federation of Music Clubs. She remains committed to professional growth and excellence in music education, while striving to create engaging, personalized lessons that foster confidence, creativity and a lifelong love of music for students of all ages.
