Originally from Northern Illinois, Kirsten Swed has over a decade of experience as a church pianist, choral accompanist, and piano teacher.

In addition to teaching beginner and intermediate piano students in the conservatory, Kirsten also serves as the accompanist for the Reformation Chorale of Reformation Bible College and the graded children’s choir program at Saint Andrew’s Chapel.
