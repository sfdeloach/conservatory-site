Tuition is due on or before the first day of the semester. Payments received after the due date incur a {{< handbook_value "fees.late" >}} late fee, and no lessons are given until payment has been made. Lesson rates and fees are on the [Registration](/registration/) page.

Credit cards, checks, and cash are accepted.

## Pay online

Pay tuition by credit or debit card. A processing fee is added to online card payments to cover their cost; the form shows the amount. Installment payments are not available for the summer semester.

{{< destination_button "fullPayment" "installmentPayment" >}}

## Pay by check

Make the check payable to Saint Andrew’s Conservatory, write “Lesson Payment” on it, and mail it to {{< contact "mailing" >}}.

## Support the Scholarship Fund

Help us train the next generation of musicians. Tax-deductible gifts of any amount to the Conservatory Scholarship Fund may be made online or in person.
