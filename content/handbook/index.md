## Mission

The Saint Andrew’s Conservatory of Music exists to train musicians for the church, the community, and the world, for the glory of God.

## Registration

- Each potential student, whether new or returning, must complete a registration form on the [registration page](/registration/). Registrations may only be done online. Due to limited studio openings, submission of a new student registration does not guarantee enrollment in any studio. Disclaimer: Unless you register, there is no guarantee that a spot will be held for you in your teacher’s studio.
- If the office does not receive payment before Friday at 5pm of the first week of lessons, you will be turned away from your next lesson and a {{< handbook_value "fees.late" >}} late fee will be added.
- There is a one-time non-refundable {{< handbook_value "fees.registration" >}} fee for the initial setup of lessons for a student. An additional {{< handbook_value "fees.change" >}} fee will be incurred if a student adds an additional lesson type or makes significant changes to the lesson schedule. Additional lessons or schedule changes require staff coordination with the teacher(s), as well as accounting and facility arrangements.
- Registration fees are waived for students who return within one year.
- Advanced students may be required to play a prepared piece, sight-read an unfamiliar piece of music, and/or meet with the individual faculty member to discuss goals before being admitted to study.

### Tuition

Tuition will be due on or before the first day of the semester. The tuition for a full semester will be assessed to all students registering, regardless of what date they begin. Students who pay in full on or before the first day of the semester will have the fee reduced by the amount of one lesson. There is no prepayment discount for the summer semester. A convenient payment plan is also available for those who prefer to pay monthly with an additional {{< handbook_value "fees.paymentPlan" >}} fee per month. ### Payment

Payment by credit card, check, or cash must be made on or before the published due date. If paying by check, please make the check out to Saint Andrew’s Conservatory. Payments received after the due date will incur a {{< handbook_value "fees.late" >}} late fee. No lessons will be given to the student until payment has been made.

Students who withdraw after the registration deadline will incur a {{< handbook_value "fees.withdrawal" >}} fee. Please see the [Withdrawal Policy](#withdrawal) for more information.

### Scholarships

## Policies

### Child safety and supervision

During lessons with minor children (students under the age of 18), at least one adult guardian (18+) in addition to the teacher should remain present in the same room throughout the duration of the lesson. This applies to all Saint Andrew’s Chapel events. If the guardian of the child must leave the room for any reason, the teacher should pause the lesson and will only resume once the guardian has returned. During lessons in which multiple-child families are present, children must remain supervised at all times.

**Under no circumstances may a Saint Andrew’s Conservatory teacher conduct a Saint Andrew’s Conservatory lesson, makeup lesson, studio class, or any other Conservatory-related event off campus. Lessons or classes of any kind are strictly prohibited in the teacher’s home, the student’s home, or any other location outside our facilities at 5525 Wayside Drive.**

### Attendance

Once a student is registered for a lesson, they are responsible for notifying the teacher if they will be tardy/absent.

- **Tardiness:** Students are to arrive on time for their lesson; if they are late they will not be guaranteed their full lesson time.
- **Excused absence:** One excused absence is permitted each semester. Notice must be given to the teacher at least 24 hours in advance. Teachers are not obligated to make up for more than one absence. Make-up lessons will usually be scheduled for the included “make-up week” in the posted schedule. Two additional absences may be excused, but it is up to the discretion of the teacher whether to schedule additional make-up lessons. This may eliminate a scheduled “break week” for the student. Refunds for missed lessons will not be issued unless waived by the Dean of the Conservatory.
- **Unexcused absence:** Cancellation on the day of a lesson may be necessary in some cases; but this will be counted as an unexcused absence. This absence may be made up at the discretion of the teacher, as they are not required to make up an unexcused absence.
- **Teacher absence:** If a teacher misses a lesson for any reason, the teacher has until the end of the semester to make up that absence. If by the end of the semester the teacher has not been able to make up their absence(s), the student will be refunded for the teacher’s missed lesson(s).

### Virtual lessons

### Recitals

#### Accompaniment

If your child’s piece requires piano accompaniment, the Conservatory has a number of accompanists that you may contact and arrange compensation privately for a rehearsal and the recital. Please reach out to the music administrative assistant as soon as you know you need a recital accompanist. NOTE: Not all instructors are able to accompany their students and your accompanist must be approved by the Dean of the Conservatory prior to making any arrangements.

#### Etiquette

Students must be appropriately dressed for a formal recital. Their attire should reflect the high standards that the Conservatory upholds. Casual clothing should be avoided.

Students will sit together in the front rows. This is so they are close enough to approach the stage efficiently. In the case of very young children, sitting with parents is permitted.

Students should remain until the completion of the recital and must sit quietly and listen attentively to other students as they perform.

Teachers will instruct their students in correct etiquette for approaching and leaving their instrument and receiving applause.

Students are encouraged to perform their piece(s) by memory, if possible.

### Music copying

Copying copyrighted music is illegal. While copyrights vary by publishing year, any music written or published after 1923 is likely still under copyright. Even when older music is in the public domain, the sheet music itself (if created by a publisher after 1923) is not, unless specified by the publisher. Since 1989 the © symbol/information has been optional, so even if you do not see the copyright information printed on the music, it may still be under copyright. Music no longer in print may still be under copyright; therefore, express written permission by the publisher must be obtained in order to copy out-of-print music.

Faculty and students of Saint Andrew’s Conservatory will not be permitted to make or use copies of copyrighted music, unless express written permission from the publisher allows them to make a temporary copy until purchased music arrives (at which point copied music must be destroyed). The facilities of Saint Andrew’s Conservatory will not be made available for students wishing to copy music. Faculty may copy proven non-copyrighted music with the assistance of Saint Andrew’s Chapel staff.

Copies or excerpts with sources properly cited may be made in compliance with “fair use” laws (Title 17, Section 107, U.S. Code)—that is, short excerpts or exhibits in an academic paper, speech, article, classroom demonstration, or similar.

### Facilities maintenance

The facilities of Saint Andrew’s Conservatory are provided courtesy of Saint Andrew’s Chapel. Facilities and supplies should remain as they were before use of the classrooms. Please adhere to the following guidelines:

- Leave toilet facilities and play areas as you found them.
- Do not use church supplies intended for Sunday use, including food, drinks, and craft supplies.
- Do not allow children to run in the sanctuary or fellowship hall, or play with church items and supplies.

### Student practice

Conservatory students may reserve through the Music Administrative Assistant up to 3 hours of practice time. Rooms with a piano will be assigned based on availability. The sanctuary organ has a limit of 1 hour per week, so other organ practice times will be on the narthex organ. Practice is not allowed on the sanctuary piano.

### Dismissal

Saint Andrew’s Conservatory reserves the right to dismiss any student due to frequent tardiness and/or absence, behavioral problems, nonpayment, or noncompliance with the policies outlined in this handbook.

## Summer study

Teachers and students will schedule six or eight lessons within the dates of the summer semester (see the [academic calendar](#academic-calendar)). This allows for flexible availability for both teacher and student.

Each summer, we also offer a five-day Summer Music Camp, for students who have completed grades K–4, and Summer Music Academy, for intermediate to advanced musicians who have completed grades 5–12. Academy sessions are taught by contracted teachers who are confessing Christians and who take their mission as teachers and performers seriously.
