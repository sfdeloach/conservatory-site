## Year-round instruction in seven disciplines — for students of every age and stage.

> If music is, as Martin Luther says, a gift from God — then we are obliged to develop that gift.

Saint Andrew's is a performing-arts community that balances weekly lessons with seasonal concerts and recitals. Our instructors meet each student with a developmentally appropriate education in a nurturing, Christ-centered context.

## Seven disciplines, one calling

### Piano

From first scales to advanced repertoire — three concert grands and dedicated studios. The heart of the Conservatory, open to students of every age and stage.

### Organ

Study on two pipe organs in one of Central Florida's premier sacred venues — a rare opportunity for serious keyboardists to master the king of instruments.

### Harpsichord

Historically informed performance on the Conservatory's harpsichord — Baroque literature, continuo, and ornamentation with a specialist faculty.

### Violin & Viola

Tone, technique, and musicianship in the string tradition, with chamber and recital opportunities throughout the year for players of all levels.

### Cello & Bass

The lower strings, from foundational bowing to concerto repertoire — grounded in ensemble playing and a warm studio culture.

### Voice

Classical vocal training in breath, diction, and interpretation — art song, oratorio, and sacred repertoire, with regular performance opportunities.
