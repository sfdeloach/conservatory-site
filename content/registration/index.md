Every student, new or returning, registers online each semester. Studio openings are limited, so a new student registration does not guarantee a place in a studio. Unless you register, no spot is held for you in your teacher’s studio.

{{< destination_button "semesterRegistration" >}}

Registration dates for each semester are on the [Schedule](/schedule/). The [Student Handbook](/handbook/) has the complete policies and standards of the Conservatory.

Summer Music Camp and Summer Music Academy have their own registration, on the [Summer Academy](/summer-academy/) page.

## Registration fees

- A one-time, non-refundable {{< handbook_value "fees.registration" >}} fee covers the initial setup of lessons for a student.
- An additional {{< handbook_value "fees.change" >}} fee applies when a student adds a lesson type or makes significant changes to the lesson schedule.
- Registration fees are waived for students who return within one year.
- Returning students who want priority placement should tell the Conservatory Office before the end of the current semester.

## Tuition

{{< lesson_rates >}}

- Tuition for the full semester is charged to every student who registers, whatever date they begin.
- Students who pay in full on or before the first day of the semester have their tuition reduced by the cost of one lesson. There is no prepayment discount for the summer semester.
- A payment plan is available for those who prefer to pay monthly, with an additional {{< handbook_value "fees.paymentPlan" >}} fee per month.
- Members of Saint Andrew’s Chapel receive a {{< handbook_value "discounts.member" >}} discount.
- Payments received after the due date incur a {{< handbook_value "fees.late" >}} late fee. No lessons are given until payment has been made.

See [Payment](/payment/) for how to pay.

## Withdrawal

Withdrawal requests must be made in writing, including by email. A {{< handbook_value "fees.withdrawal" >}} fee is collected for any withdrawal, unless waived by the Dean of the Conservatory. Refunds follow this schedule:

Tuition balance is the tuition remaining for lessons not taken. See the [Withdrawal policy](/handbook/#withdrawal) in the Student Handbook.

## Scholarships

Outstanding students who are recommended by their teacher after at least one full year of study may be considered for a {{< handbook_value "discounts.scholarship" >}} scholarship. Teachers contact the Conservatory Office to request an application form.

Help us train the next generation of musicians. Tax-deductible gifts of any amount to the Conservatory Scholarship Fund may be made online or in person.
