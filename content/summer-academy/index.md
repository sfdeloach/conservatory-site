Summer Music Camp and Academy at Saint Andrew’s Conservatory are unique opportunities to combine fun with musical exploration. Students have the chance to participate in music ensembles and refine their musical skills. They are mentored and taught by caring instructors who are exceptional teachers and performers on various instruments. Participants learn about music in a fun environment where God is honored and glorified.

Elementary school students get to participate in live performance on a variety of instruments. More advanced students in middle and high school participate in orchestra and chamber ensembles under the instruction of our skilled faculty. To the delight of parents and friends, Camp and Academy students perform a final concert at the end of the week.

---

## Summer Music Camp

### Dates for 2027 coming soon

For rising 1st through 5th graders, no music experience necessary.

Students will explore music in a group context with their peers as they engage with choral music, ORFF instruments, handbells, and a variety of other musical activities.

## Summer Music Academy

### Dates for 2027 coming soon

For rising 6th through graduated high school seniors with instrumental experience (audition video required, including for returning students). We offer ensemble experiences for strings, winds, and keyboard instruments.

Each student participates in orchestra, in addition to smaller chamber ensembles organized by ability. Each ensemble has instruction time with a different faculty member each day throughout the week, which gives students a broad experience with different coaching perspectives from our experienced faculty.

The week ends with a final concert for family and friends in the sanctuary.

Registration is pending until your audition video has been adjudicated. An audition does not guarantee admittance. Accepted students will receive music assignments before the Academy week and should learn their parts as thoroughly as possible before it begins.

Details of the audition requirements, including video submission instructions, are on the registration form.
